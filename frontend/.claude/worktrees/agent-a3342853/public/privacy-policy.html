🎓 TOEFL Quiz — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TOEFL Quiz ("we," "our," or "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using TOEFL Quiz, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account: Email address, display name (if you create an account)
  • Quiz Data: Your responses, scores, and learning progress
  • Feedback: Support requests you submit

2.2 Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Features used, time spent, learning patterns
  • Device Info: Device type, OS version, app version
  • Performance: Crash reports, error logs (for stability improvement)
  • Identifiers: Push notification token (FCM) for in-app notifications

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing Services: Running quizzes, tracking progress, generating personalized content
  • AI Features: Generating quiz questions using AI (Groq/LangChain)
  • Improving the App: Analyzing usage patterns to enhance experience
  • Communication: Sending important updates and push notifications
  • Security: Detecting and preventing fraud or abuse

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored securely on Supabase with encryption at rest and in transit (HTTPS)
  • We implement Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure users can only access their own data
  • Backups are disabled on Android to prevent ADB data extraction
  • No method of transmission is 100% secure; however, we employ industry-standard practices

6. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Quiz Data: Retained for progress tracking; deletable on request
  • Analytics: Aggregated and anonymized after 90 days
  • Crash Reports: Retained for 90 days in Sentry

7. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact: support@toeflquiz.app

8. Children's Privacy

TOEFL Quiz is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, please contact us immediately.

9. Offline Functionality

TOEFL Quiz can function offline. Data generated during offline use is synchronized when you reconnect. The offline queue is stored locally and not shared with third parties.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be notified via in-app notification and the "Last Updated" date above.
