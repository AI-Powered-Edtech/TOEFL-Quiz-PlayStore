1. Introduction

Welcome to TOEFL Quiz ("we," "our," or "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using TOEFL Quiz, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account: Email address, display name (if you create an account)
  • Quiz Data: Your responses, scores, and learning progress
  • Feedback: Support requests you submit

2.2 Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Features used, time spent, learning patterns
  • Device Info: Device type, OS version, app version
  • Performance: Crash reports, error logs (for stability improvement)
  • Identifiers: Push notification token (FCM) for in-app notifications

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing Services: Running quizzes, tracking progress, generating personalized content
  • AI Features: Generating quiz questions using AI (Groq/LangChain)
  • Improving the App: Analyzing usage patterns to enhance experience
  • Communication: Sending important updates and push notifications
  • Security: Detecting and preventing fraud or abuse

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored securely on Supabase with encryption at rest and in transit (HTTPS)
  • We implement Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure users can only access their own data
  • Backups are disabled on Android to prevent ADB data extraction
  • No method of transmission is 100% secure; however, we employ industry-standard practices

6. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Quiz Data: Retained for progress tracking; deletable on request
  • Analytics: Aggregated and anonymized after 90 days
  • Crash Reports: Retained for 90 days in Sentry

7. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact: support@toeflquiz.app

8. Children's Privacy

TOEFL Quiz is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, please contact us immediately.

9. Offline Functionality

TOEFL Quiz can function offline. Data generated during offline use is synchronized when you reconnect. The offline queue is stored locally and not shared with third parties.

12. GDPR Rights EU Users

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 grants you the rights below in relation to your personal data.

Data Controller

The data controller for TOEFL Quiz is the operator of the App. Contact the controller at: privacy@toeflquiz.example.

Your Rights

  • Right of access — obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your data.
  • Right to restriction of processing — limit how we use your data.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time.

How to Exercise These Rights

  • In the App, open Settings → Account & Data → Export my data to download a copy of your personal data.
  • Delete your account in-app via Settings → Account & Data → Delete my account, which calls DELETE /api/auth/account and permanently removes your data within 24 hours.
  • For any other request, email privacy@toeflquiz.example. We respond within 30 days.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe your rights have been violated, you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (for example, the Data Protection Authority in your EU member state, or the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the United Kingdom).

13. CCPA Rights California Users

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the CPRA, grants you the following rights.

Categories of Personal Information We Collect

  • Identifiers: email address, username, device identifiers, FCM push token.
  • Customer records: display name, profile photo.
  • Commercial information: subscription status and purchase history (via RevenueCat / Google Play).
  • Internet activity: app usage, features accessed, quiz responses, crash logs.
  • Inferences: learning progress and estimated proficiency level.

Your Rights

  • Right to know — what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • Right to delete — request deletion of personal information we have collected.
  • Right to correct — request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt-out of sale or sharing — We do NOT sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to non-discrimination — we will not deny services, charge different prices, or provide a different quality of service because you exercised your CCPA rights.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@toeflquiz.example or use the in-app Account & Data tools. We will verify your identity before fulfilling the request.

14. UU PDP Pengguna Indonesia

Bagi pengguna di Indonesia, perlindungan data pribadi Anda diatur oleh Undang-Undang No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Perlindungan Data Pribadi (UU PDP).

Hak Subjek Data

  • Hak perbaikan: meminta perbaikan data yang tidak akurat.
  • Hak penghapusan: meminta penghapusan data pribadi dan akun Anda.
  • Hak menarik persetujuan: menarik persetujuan pemrosesan data sewaktu-waktu.
  • Hak membatasi pemrosesan dan hak mengajukan keberatan atas pemrosesan tertentu.
  • Hak portabilitas: menerima data Anda dalam format yang dapat digunakan kembali.

Pengendali Data

Pengendali Data Pribadi ("Data Controller") untuk TOEFL Quiz adalah operator Aplikasi. Hubungi kami di privacy@toeflquiz.example untuk menggunakan hak Anda.

Pengaduan

Jika Anda merasa hak Anda dilanggar, Anda dapat mengajukan pengaduan kepada Kementerian Komunikasi dan Informatika (KEMKOMINFO) Republik Indonesia atau lembaga pengawas Perlindungan Data Pribadi yang berwenang.

Retensi Data

Setelah akun Anda dihapus, kami menyimpan catatan terbatas (log audit, catatan transaksi) selama 3 (tiga) tahun untuk keperluan hukum, audit, dan kewajiban perpajakan, sesuai peraturan perundang-undangan yang berlaku. Setelah periode tersebut, catatan akan dianonimkan atau dihapus secara permanen.
