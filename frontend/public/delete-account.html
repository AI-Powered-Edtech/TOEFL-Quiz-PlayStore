We respect your right to control your personal data. You have two ways to permanently delete your TOEFL Quiz account and the data associated with it.

Account deletion is permanent. Once processed, your data cannot be recovered.

Option 1: Self-service (easiest)

  1. Open the TOEFL Quiz app
  2. Go to More → Settings → Account & Data
  3. Tap "Delete my account"
  4. Confirm by typing DELETE-<your-username>
  5. All your data will be permanently removed within 24 hours

Option 2: Email request

Email us at privacy@toeflquiz.example with the subject "Delete my account". Include:

  • Your username or account email
  • Confirmation that you understand the deletion is permanent

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm via email once complete.

What gets deleted

  • Profile, username, email, avatar
  • Quiz history, essays, vocabulary collections
  • Notifications, social circles, friendships
  • Purchase records (retained 7 years for tax / audit purposes as required by law)

What does NOT get deleted

  • Anonymized analytics (aggregated, non-identifying)
  • Transaction records with Google Play (managed by Google — see Google Play support)

Questions?

If you need help or have questions about the deletion process, contact privacy@toeflquiz.example. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for more information.
